About Gymnovite

Gymnovite Philosophy

What if healing begins with information?

Every experience leaves an imprint. Your brain, body, emotions, and experiences constantly shape how you move, feel, and respond to the world.

At Gymnovite, we don't see strength, movement, and healing as separate. We see them as different ways of helping the body receive the information it needs to feel safe enough to change.

  • Strength Training restores efficient movement.
  • Neuro Training restores the brain's sensory input.
  • Treatments support restoration through Reiki and sound therapy.

Together, they create one philosophy: working with the body's own capacity to adapt, restore, and return to balance.

Christina Lövald Hellberg

Founder of Gymnovite

As a former elite athlete and coach, I spent years searching for better ways to improve movement, recover from injury, and unlock human potential.

After experiencing injuries myself, I realized that lasting change isn't created by strengthening muscles alone. Our movement is shaped by our biomechanics, our nervous system, and the experiences that influence how safe our bodies feel.

Today, I combine science-based strength training, neuro training, and energy healing modalities into one integrated approach. My goal is to help you move with greater ease, build lasting strength, and reconnect with your body's natural ability to heal.

Because true transformation begins when the body, brain, and mind work together.
